- [Blank measurement schema](experiments/measurement_template.csv): no fabricated production rows.

The founder recalls 400 variants over four weeks with failure-based QC/repair choices informed by model assessments. This is explicitly reported memory. Logs, outcome denominators, costs, historical model revisions, and the meaning of the four-week interval have not been verified.

The observed current development catalog contains 12 video and 7 image entries. Its presence is evidence of configuration, not a historical run or comparative model ability. Other modalities need their own verified action registry.

## What the calculations establish

The reference code reproduces constructed examples and checks specified mathematical properties. It does not generate videos, benchmark providers, run a complete agent, or certify production quality. Probability intervals and cost units in the simulations are invented inputs. Predictive scenarios are not historical estimates.

The four manuscripts share one research program and one case-study lead. Shared evidence is not independent replication. Novelty and the appropriate publication unit require assessment against prior work; a consolidated paper may be appropriate. Strong theoretical work can be publishable without an empirical benchmark, but these drafts do not claim a new core statistical or control theorem.
